## Recommendations

1. **Prioritize Fruits & Vegetables for improvement:** it has the lowest delivered revenue at INR 9,790 and a target shortfall of INR 2,210, so it is the clearest category for additional catalog or marketing attention.
2. **Review Snacks & Beverages performance:** it generates INR 10,895 against a INR 13,000 target, a shortfall of INR 2,105, placing it in the critical tier and making it a priority for supplier/category review.
